Today's Agenda

  • You've made your First Flight — a small arcade game, start to finish
  • Now it's time to chart a longer course
  • Brainstorm the kind of application you want to build
  • Pick a direction and start sketching it out

From Arcade to Ambition

  • Your arcade game proved you can go from idea to working code with an AI partner
  • Your real project doesn't have to be a game at all
  • The next several slides are one possible category each — not a menu you must pick from, just fuel for brainstorming (which I hope you do in CardStorming)
  • If you already know what you want to build, skip ahead and start sketching it

Video Game

  • A bigger or more original game than your First Flight arcade clone
  • Platformer, puzzle game, idle/incremental game, or your own twist on a classic
  • Good fit if: you like fast feedback loops and don't mind sweating the small stuff — physics, timing, "game feel"

Productivity Tool

  • Something that helps you get things done
  • Note-taking app, habit tracker, daily journal, focus/pomodoro timer
  • Good fit if: you already have a personal workflow you wish worked better

Collaboration Tool

  • Something multiple people use together
  • Shared to-do list, team brainstorming board (like CardStorming — the very tool you'll use today), shared calendar or scheduler
  • Good fit if: you're solving a problem you've hit with friends, family, or teammates

Personal Finance Tracker

  • Budget tracker, expense splitter for shared costs, savings-goal tracker
  • Good fit if: numbers and simple math motivate you

Data Science Project

  • Pull in a dataset — sports stats, weather, prices, your own tracked habits — and look for patterns in it
  • Could end up as a chart, a dashboard, or just a script that answers a question you're curious about
  • Good fit if: you like to measure and understand how the world actually works

Learning & Study Tool

  • Flashcards with spaced repetition, a quiz generator, a language-practice drill
  • Good fit if: you like teaching yourself things, or want to help someone else learn

Creative Tool

  • Generative art, a simple music/beat maker, a writing-prompt generator, a mood board
  • Good fit if: you want the AI to become your creative sandbox partner

Utility / Life-Hack Tool

  • Recipe box and meal planner, packing-list generator, unit converter, a "decide for me" random picker
  • Good fit if: you have a small recurring annoyance you want to automate away

Community Tool

  • Small forum, event/meetup coordinator, neighborhood recommendation board
  • Good fit if: you want to bring a group of people together around something

Course & Presentation Tool

  • Slide-deck generator, course-outline planner, study-guide builder — something meta, like this very class site
  • Good fit if: you're curious how a tool like the one running this deck actually works, and want to build your own version

Mobile App

  • A native or cross-platform app for iPhone, iPad, or Android
  • Could be a mobile-first version of any idea above — a game, a tracker, a collaboration tool
  • Good fit if: you want it living on a home screen, or need device features like camera, location, or notifications

Something Completely Different

  • It doesn't have to be an app at all — if the AI can read and edit the file format, it can help you build with it
  • Example: design a circuit board in KiCad — schematics and PCB layouts are just text files under the hood, the same way code is
  • Good fit if: you want to learn something new outside pure software, as long as what you're working with is text-based

Your Far Horizons Activity

  • Skim back over the categories — or bring an idea that's entirely your own
  • Brainstorm specific ideas in CardStorming
  • Narrow down to the one idea you're most excited to build
  • Give it a name, write a short description, and list must-have features
  • We'll come back to this project in later sessions
