# The Story of Saving Your Work

## The lost afternoon

Maya spent an afternoon rewriting her game's menu. Then she asked the AI for "one small tweak" and the whole menu vanished. There was no undo, and the old version was gone.

She stared at the screen for a long minute. Every version of her work had been overwritten by the next one, and nothing remembered what the menu used to look like.

## The save point

A friend told her about **Git**, which takes a snapshot of a project whenever you ask it to. Each snapshot is called a *commit*.

- A commit is a save point you can return to
- A branch is a safe place to try an idea
- GitHub keeps a copy online

> Think of commits as checkpoints in a video game.

Next time, Maya made a commit before the tweak. When the menu vanished again, she rolled back in seconds.
